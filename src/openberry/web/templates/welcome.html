Free · self-hosted · open source

Find buyers the moment they show intent.

OpenBerry watches free public signals (people posting about your topic, engaging with competitors, hiring, raising money), scores every lead against your ideal customer, and lets Claude research them and write the outreach. Nothing is sent until it is approved: by you, or by auto-approve if you turn it on.

    Register

    Fill in the registration board: what you sell, who buys it, and which buying signals matter. About five minutes. Or let Claude interview you and fill it in.

    Collect signals

    Free sources out of the box: Hacker News, GitHub, Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job boards, Google News, RSS and SEC EDGAR. Also Google Maps businesses (with your API key). Reddit works once the server has Reddit API access.

  3. 3

    Let Claude work

    Connect Claude through MCP to qualify leads, find decision-makers and draft personal messages for you to approve. Send them yourself, or let OpenBerry email them from your own mailbox (off until you turn it on).
